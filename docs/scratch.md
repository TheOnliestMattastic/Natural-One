## Game Loop

0. Roll for starting stats
1. Perform actions
	a. Forage: -1 action
	b. Craft: -1 action; roll for success
2. Draw Night card
	a. Encounters
	b. Debuffs
3. Apply upkeep damage
	a. -1 Thirst & Hunger
